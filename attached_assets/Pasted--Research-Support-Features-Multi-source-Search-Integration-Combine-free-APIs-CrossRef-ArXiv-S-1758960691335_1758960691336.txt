🔍 Research Support Features

Multi-source Search Integration

Combine free APIs (CrossRef, ArXiv, Semantic Scholar, CORE) to give broader coverage.

Ranking system: prioritize most cited / recent / peer-reviewed.

Research Outline Auto-Builder

From search results, generate structured outlines:

Introduction

Literature Review

Methodology

Results/Findings

Discussion

Conclusion

Future Work

Contextual Summarization

Summaries in different modes: short notes, paragraph overview, key points.

Highlight methodology vs. results separately for clarity.

📝 Writing & Drafting Tools

Research Paper Draft Generator

Auto-generate a starter draft with placeholders for student’s own data.

Example: “Insert your experiment results here.”

Academic Tone Rewriter

Rewrite casual text → formal academic writing.

Suggest synonyms, formal phrases, and citations.

Plagiarism & Paraphrasing Assistant

Use open-source similarity checkers.

Suggest alternative wording for flagged content.

Citation Manager

Auto-generate APA/MLA/IEEE references.

Export to BibTeX, EndNote, or Zotero.

Note-Taking Workspace

Export Options

Smart Templates

Thesis

AI Research Mentor Mode
